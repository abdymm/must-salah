# Must Salah Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Must Salah is a macOS menu bar app that shows prayer times. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer does not collect, receive or share your data.

## What the app uses

**Location.** If you allow location access, your approximate location (about 1 km accuracy) is used to calculate prayer times on your Mac. Location access is optional: you can deny it and enter a city instead.

**City lookup.** To find your city's name, or a city you search for, the app uses Apple's geocoding service. Your approximate location or the city you typed is sent to Apple for this lookup and handled under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). It is not sent to the developer.

**Notifications.** Prayer alerts are scheduled and delivered on your Mac.

**Adhan audio.** If you choose an audio file, a copy is stored inside the app's private folder on your Mac. It is not uploaded anywhere.

## What is stored, and how to delete it

Your settings, last known coordinates, city name and chosen adhan file are stored only on your Mac, for as long as the app keeps them. The developer has no access to them.

To delete them, remove the app and its data from your Mac. You can turn off location access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
